The project contains a Lean 4 proof that the Cayley graph of the symmetric
group Sₙ with generators L, R = L⁻¹, and X has diameter n(n−1)/2 for every
n ≥ 4. The proof and formalization were developed with assistance from
OpenAI Codex. The mathematical claims are stated explicitly in the Lean
sources; reproducible verification instructions are in the README.

The underlying problem and earlier computations predate this project.
See [OEIS A186783](https://oeis.org/A186783) and its references.
The repository uses Lean 4 and Mathlib; their authors retain credit for
those tools and dependencies.
